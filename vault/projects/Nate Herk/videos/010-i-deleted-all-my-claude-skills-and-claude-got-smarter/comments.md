## @psiutube

When I hear Boris saying we should cut 80% of the instructions, I think there’s a risk of turning an interesting insight from an Anthropic engineer into a general engineering principle.

Of course model builders want us to rely more on the model itself. But the more your system depends on how a specific model behaves today, the more you end up following the algorithm.

We’ve seen this pattern before. People optimize for the current algorithm, get some short-term gains, then the algorithm changes and they have to adapt again.

I’d rather build around principles that survive model generations, even if that takes more work upfront. Models will keep changing. Good engineering should not have to change every time they do.

Maybe the real advantage is not learning how to follow the algorithm better, but building something that doesn’t depend on following it.
