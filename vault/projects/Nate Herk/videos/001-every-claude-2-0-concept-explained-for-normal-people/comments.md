## @versesofrobin

Hey Nate! Love your explanations and the constant updates. I’d like to suggest a concept for a video: Thinking Literacy.

Working with people, I keep seeing the same gap. Everyone has Claude, and Claude can execute almost any plan. But when someone wants to build an agent for a specific goal, say, a book-publishing agent, they don’t know how to find the steps in the first place. They ask Claude, it lists steps or gives options A/B/C, and they just pick one. You can’t evaluate an option you never thought of yourself. People are outsourcing the thinking, not just the doing.

Lots of creators teach tools. Honestly, that’s the easy part now. What’s missing is teaching how to break a fuzzy goal into pieces, see how the pieces connect, and know what to ask for.

A real example: I want to make illustrated retellings of Plato’s dialogues (public domain), using Claude to animate the hard philosophical ideas. I know what I want. I don’t know how to think my way to a piece-by-piece setup.

Would love to see a video where you take a messy real goal like this and think it through live, from zero, showing the questions you ask yourself along the way.

What do you think?
