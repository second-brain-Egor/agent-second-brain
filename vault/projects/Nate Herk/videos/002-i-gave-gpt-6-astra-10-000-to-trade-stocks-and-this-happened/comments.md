## @SolutionsJoe

Day trading isn't a video game—here’s the brutal reality: 🛑

The $25K Rule & Trade Limit: If you execute 4 or more day trades in 5 days, you trigger Pattern Day Trader rules requiring a $25,000 minimum in a margin account.

Borrowed Money: Margin gives you leverage, meaning you're trading with borrowed broker funds to amplify your bets.

You Can Lose MORE Than You Put In: Unlike standard investing where you only lose your stake, margin trading can leave you actually owing your broker real money if a trade tanks.

Know the risks before playing with fire. 🔥📉

Now, I am not completely against using some new cool tech to try and make some money in the financial markets but, far safer option is through something like Kalshi probability market.

Also, if you want to do some swing trading and have an AI agent help pick those investment options that might move say 5 or 10 percent over the next few days or week; much safer because it should not require a margin account.

This not investment advice, always do your own research or consult a licensed professional  ... have fun but, don't risk what you can't afford to lose!
