## @myboringdesktop

One-shotting an app is NOT the most efficient method. Try planning the app first. Then work the plan. One-shot features, not apps. Doing this does keep the human in the loop a lot more, but these models are NOT ready to one-shot entire apps unless those apps are extremely simple or you have no real idea of the end product and whatever it produces is fine. Develop apps feature by feature. You'll use fewer tokens and the model will not wander as much. Building a feature at a time allows independent testing of each feature and allows the developer to catch model drift and to adjust if the dev wants to course correct without an entire rebuild and using tons more tokens. Even when models get better at one-shotting apps, you will still need to re-work it again and again because we don't even know everything the app needs or should do. We start with a general idea and refine during development. Trying to one-shot an app skips that in-line-development and is sure to disappoint. Great video though!
